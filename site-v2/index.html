A cup worth slowing down for.

Mr. Bean is a small coffee bar for people who notice the details: the bloom on a fresh pour, the weight of a warm cup, the quiet before the city wakes up.

We brew Indian single-origins from Araku and Chikmagalur, pull every shot by hand, and keep the menu short so everything on it is worth ordering. Come for the first cup at six. Stay for the second.

Every cup starts long before the pour.

  1. Sourced by season

    Single-origin beans from estates in Araku Valley and Chikmagalur, chosen for what is tasting best right now.

  2. Roasted in small batches

    Light enough to keep the fruit, rested for a week so every shot pulls sweet and even.

  3. Poured to order

    Ground fresh for each cup and brewed by hand. Nothing waits on a warmer.
