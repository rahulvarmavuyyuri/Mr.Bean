Our story

A green corner for coffee, done the slow way.

Mr. Bean began the way most good things do — with a kettle, a notebook of recipes, and a few leafy plants by a window in Bhimavaram. We roast carefully, pour by hand, and plate with the same attention a home-cooked meal deserves.

Sit under the trellis. Watch the morning turn gold. Order a second cup. It is that kind of place.
